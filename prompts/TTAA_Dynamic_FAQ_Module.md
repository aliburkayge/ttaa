# TTAA Dynamic FAQ Module

This module applies to every generated FAQ section. The article title is the boundary: infer the exact service, language pair, document, formal process and visitor intent without exposing the analysis.

## Required output

- Write 7-10 natural customer questions.
- The first question normally explains how TTAA helps with the exact topic.
- Include 2-3 TTAA service questions; at least two questions explicitly name TTAA.
- Include 2-3 questions unique to the exact language pair, document or process.
- Include no more than 1-2 formal-service questions unless the title itself is about certification, notarization, apostille, legalization or attestation.
- Include two transaction questions. At least one explains what to send for document review or quotation.
- Answers normally contain 45-90 words and provide the useful answer in the first one or two sentences.

## Topic rules

For language-pair pages, cover realities of that pair: both directions, names and transliteration, regional or writing-system variants, stamps, tables, terminology, dates and reference spelling where relevant. For document pages, keep most questions on that document. For formal-process pages, explain the exact process without claiming that one sequence applies universally.

At least 60% of a language-pair FAQ section must remain about the pair, documents, accuracy, TTAA service, turnaround, quotation or delivery. On non-formal pages, no more than 30% of questions may focus mainly on notarization, apostille, legalization or authorities.

## Questions to avoid

Do not default to broad questions such as “Is translation always required?”, “Why is translation important?”, “Which source should I trust?”, “What is translation?”, “Is professional translation necessary?” or “How long will the process take?”. Use a service-specific turnaround question instead.

Do not repeat the same answer under several versions of “What should I send?”, “Can TTAA review my document?” and “How do I start?”. Do not turn a language-service FAQ into a general legalization or government-procedure guide.

## TTAA and safety

Describe concrete work: document review, direction confirmation, appropriate translator assignment, names/dates/numbers/terminology checks, revision, relevant certification coordination, realistic timing after review, and digital or physical delivery options.

Never claim guaranteed acceptance, universal legal validity, mandatory notarization/apostille, a universal sequence or guaranteed same-day delivery. Prefer “may be required”, “depending on the receiving institution”, “where applicable”, “requirements vary”, “subject to document review” and “TTAA can assist with coordination”.

The generator must silently audit customer-intent match, TTAA presence, topic specificity, conversion support, topic drift, repetition and practical value. Planning notes and audit scores never appear in the article.
